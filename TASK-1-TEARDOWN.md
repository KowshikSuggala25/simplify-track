## What I checked

- Scrolled back through multiple months.
- Searched for a merchant.
- Opened a transaction.
- Pulled to refresh.
- Tested the screen with the network disabled and restored.

## Sample observations to replace

1. **Fast:** The first visible list appeared quickly after the account was connected.
2. **Stutter:** Loading an older month caused the list to jump while more content arrived.
3. **Confusing:** Debit and credit values were visually similar, making direction hard to scan.
4. **Confusing:** A refresh did not make it clear whether the full history or only the visible page was current.

## Three changes I would make

1. Use distinct debit/credit treatment and show monthly spend and income together.
2. Keep the last successful list visible during refresh and show a small offline/currentness state.
3. Keep the transaction detail context available without taking the user away from the month they were browsing.
